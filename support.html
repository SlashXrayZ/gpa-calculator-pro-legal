Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my GPA?

Simply add your courses with their grades and credit hours, and the app will automatically calculate your GPA.

What grading scales are supported?

The app supports both the US 4.0 scale and the Australian 7.0 scale. You can switch between them in settings.

How do I restore my premium subscription?

Go to the Premium screen and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription will be restored if it is linked to your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions. Find GPA Calculator Pro and tap Cancel Subscription.
